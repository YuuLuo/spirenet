# SpireHost fidelity ledger - combat team fragment (H1)

> **MERGED into `host\FIDELITY.md` at the H1 integration (2026-11-15).** This fragment is kept for history. It describes the
> team's stand-alone build against the stage-0 snapshot and is no longer checked against the registry: ids, counts,
> switches and "temporary" / "shim" remarks below may be out of date. The merged ledger is the source of truth
> (its section 4 lists what was removed or moved at the integration).

Owner: combat team (`SpireHost.Driver.Combat`). Same format and rules as `host\FIDELITY.md`; the lead merges this
fragment into it. Every entry is registered through `PatchRegistry` by `CombatModule.Register`
(`CombatScheduling.Install`, `SelectionCapture.Install`, `IntentTracker.Install`, three `PatchRegistry.Note`
calls), fails closed at registration (a missing target throws at boot; `SelectionCapture.Install` additionally
throws unless exactly 15 captures were installed; the transpiler throws unless it replaced exactly 1 call) and is
listed by `SpireHost.Driver.Combat --list-patches` (`bench\h1-combat\patches_combat.json`):

**20 entries = 17 Harmony patches + 3 non-Harmony notes; Instrumentation 17, Plumbing 2, Scheduling 1, Fidelity 0,
Behaviour 0.** With this module only, `--driver-info` shows 35 core + 20 = 55 entries (Plumbing 24, Scheduling 7,
Instrumentation 21, Fidelity 3); the core's `selection-context-choose-a-card` is among the 35 (see "Integration"
at the end).

Source references are `ref\sts2-v0113-src` paths without the `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.` prefix. Report:
`notes\host-h1-combat.md`. Contract requests: `host\contract-requests\combat.md`.

**No patch of this module changes an argument, a return value or a computed result.** Sixteen Harmony patches are
read-only prefixes / postfixes. The seventeenth (`combat-end-run-save-yield`, Scheduling) changes WHEN the
remainder of one game method runs - at the next turn of the engine pump instead of inline, which is where the real
client resumes it - and nothing else. The module restores no TestMode gate (those belong to the fidelity team) and
does not touch `CardSelectCmd.FromChooseABundleScreen` (events team: gate + BUNDLE frame; asserted by the
self-test case `relic SCROLL_BOXES (bundle entry is not ours)`).
